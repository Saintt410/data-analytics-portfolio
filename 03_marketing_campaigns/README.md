# Marketing Campaign Analysis

**Business problem:** Where is marketing spend generating the strongest return?

**Required CSV columns:** `campaign, channel, campaign_type, spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, revenue`

Run: `python 03_marketing_campaigns/analysis.py [--data your.csv]` → results printed and saved to `03_marketing_campaigns/outputs/`.

The script prints *What the data tells me* findings and *What the business should do next* recommendations computed from the data.
