# Customer Churn Analysis

**Business problem:** Customers are leaving — why, and who is most at risk?

**Required CSV columns:** `customer_id, tenure, contract, payment_method, monthly_charges, tech_support, churn (0/1)`

Run: `python 02_customer_churn/analysis.py [--data your.csv]` → results printed and saved to `02_customer_churn/outputs/`.

The script prints *What the data tells me* findings and *What the business should do next* recommendations computed from the data.
